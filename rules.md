# Web Project / Portfolio Rules

I need to create a fresh, new, minimalist yet modern portfolio / project website for my portfolio. The website should be responsive and mobile-friendly.

## Color Tone

I prefer a clean color, easy readable color scheme. I prefer a color scheme that is easy on the eyes and easy to read. I prefer a color scheme that is not too dark or too light. I prefer a color scheme that is not too bold or too subtle.

## Contents

I need the website to have the following contents:

- Home
- Experience and Education
- Projects (consist of at least 1 project)
- Blog post
- Contact

### Home

This section should contains:
- A brief paragraph about my professional career
- A CV link download
- What I do (like: Software Engineering & Development, System Architecture & DevOps, Leadership and Engineering Management, Business and Product Strategy, Knowledge sharing and collaboration, Open-Source Integration)

### Experience and Education
Contains my latest uni, and timelines of my work experience
Also there should be a section to lists my skills in engineering (soft skill and hardskill)

### Projects
Contains my brief explanation of my projects, including the stacks i use

### Blog Post
Contains a thumbnail, title, brief description, and a link to the blog post

### Contact
Contains my contact information: email, phone number, and social media links

# Deployment
I need to deploy it to at least my github page (username.github.io/projects_name), or on my own domain
